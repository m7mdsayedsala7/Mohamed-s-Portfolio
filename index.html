Thought Leadership

Perspectives on
performance marketing

Media Buying
Why CPL Is a Vanity Metric If You Don't Know Lead Quality
Chasing the lowest Cost Per Lead often destroys campaign value. The brands that win are those that optimize for qualified lead rate, not raw volume. Audience segmentation and creative context are what determine whether a lead is a prospect or a time-waster.
Creative Strategy
The Performance Marketer Who Can Design Has an Unfair Advantage
Most agencies split creative and buying into separate teams. When one person understands both, how the algorithm rewards certain creative signals and how to execute that creative, the feedback loop becomes exponentially faster and the results follow.
B2B Lead Gen
Running B2B and B2C Simultaneously Requires a Different Mental Model
Franchise acquisition and retail consumer marketing operate on completely different timelines, decision journeys, and success metrics. Managing both at Ai-CHA simultaneously taught me that channel isolation, not just audience targeting, is what keeps performance clean.

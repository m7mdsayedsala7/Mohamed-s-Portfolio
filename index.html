Thought Leadership

Perspectives on
performance marketing

Media Buying
Why CPL Is a Vanity Metric If You Don't Know Lead Quality
Chasing the lowest CPL often destroys campaign value. The brands that win optimize for qualified lead rate, not raw volume — audience segmentation determines whether a lead is a prospect or a time-waster.
Creative Strategy
The Performance Marketer Who Can Design Has an Unfair Advantage
When one person understands both the algorithm and the creative, the feedback loop becomes exponentially faster. Most agencies split these roles — that gap is an advantage for those who don't.
B2B Lead Gen
Running B2B and B2C Simultaneously Requires a Different Mental Model
Franchise acquisition and consumer marketing run on different timelines and metrics. Managing both at Ai-CHA taught me that channel isolation — not just audience targeting — is what keeps performance clean.
